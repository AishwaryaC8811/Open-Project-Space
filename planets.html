Our Sun

Although this is included in the planets section of this webpage, the sun is actually not a planet.

And even though it seems huge, our sun isn't actually as big compared to other stars and is fairly young.

In the star life cycle of a sun below, you can see our star is actually only in it's second stage of life.

Conducive to life?: No, the sun is not conducive to life due to it's extreme heat.

Moons: The Sun has no moons

Rings: No rings at the moment

Namesake: Evolved from the Old English word sunne


Mercury

Contrary to popular belief, Mercury actually isn't the hottest planet in the Solar System, even if it is the closest to the Sun.

Mercury is the smallest planet in our Solar System. In fact, it is 1/3 of the Earth's size and spins perfectly upright, giving it no seasons.

Mercury spins extremely slowly. One day on Mercury is 59 Earth days. One day on Mercury is two years on it.


Conducive to life?: Due to Mercury's extremely hot days and extremely cold nights, Mercury is not conducive to life.

Moons: Mercury has no moons.

Rings: Mercury has no rings.

Namesake: Mercury was named after the Roman God of commerce and travel. (Below)
